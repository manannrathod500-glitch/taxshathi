You don't need friends or family for this. Thousands of Indian business owners ask GST
questions online every day. You show up, answer genuinely, and mention TaxSathi once.
This is how unknown products get their first users without an audience or ad budget.

**The golden rule:** answer the question fully and correctly FIRST. The TaxSathi mention
is one line at the end. Anyone who blasts links gets banned and ignored; anyone who

exactly this") — honesty converts better and follows every platform's rules.

## Where to answer (in priority order)

### 1. CAclubindia GST forum — https://www.caclubindia.com/forum/gst.asp
- India's biggest tax forum; new GST questions daily from business owners AND CAs
  (CAs are your ₹1,499/mo B2B customers too).
- Register free → browse the GST section → answer 2–3 fresh questions per week.
- Signature/profile: put taxsathi.online in your profile; in answers, mention only when relevant.

### 2. Quora — quora.com (search "GST" and follow the GST topic)
- Endless questions like "Which is the best GST billing software for a small shop?",
  "How do I file GSTR-3B myself?" — in English AND Hindi (hi.quora.com).
- Answer 3 per week with the templates below. Quora tolerates a product mention when
  the answer is genuinely useful and you disclose you built it.

### 3. Reddit — r/IndiaTax (also r/IndianBusiness, r/smallbusinessindia)
- Very active GST/ITR help threads. STRICT anti-promo culture: answer questions with
  ZERO links for the first 2–3 weeks. Put taxsathi.online in your Reddit profile bio —
  helpful answerers get profile visits. Mention the product only if someone directly
  asks "is there a tool for this?"

### 4. Facebook groups (search inside Facebook)
- Search: "Surat Textile Market", "Gujarat Business", "GST India help", "વેપારી",
  "Rajkot business". Join 5–6 groups; answer questions in Gujarati; share the Status

### 5. Telegram (search inside Telegram)
- Search: "GST India", "GST updates", "Surat textile", "tax practitioners India".

### Weekly rhythm (15–20 min/day)
- Mon/Wed/Fri: 1 CAclubindia + 1 Quora answer each day.
- Tue/Thu: Reddit + Facebook/Telegram answers.
- Every answer = your name + the same profile (photo, "Building TaxSathi AI — GST help

## What NOT to do (these get accounts banned and the domain blacklisted)
- Don't post the same text in many groups in one day (platforms detect it).
- Don't DM strangers who didn't reply to you first.
- Don't invent user counts, testimonials, or "offer ends today" urgency.
- Don't argue with skeptics — "fair point, happy to hear what would make it useful" wins.
